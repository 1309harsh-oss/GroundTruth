# Open Questions — Resolve Before Production

These are decisions requiring product owner, pilot organization or domain expert input.

1. Which first pilot category: roads, public buildings, drainage or another asset?
2. Which exact defect classes are in the first model?
3. Which project specifications, BOQ and inspection checklists are authoritative?
4. Who can create projects, milestones and evidence requirements?
5. Is GPS mandatory, optional or conditionally required per milestone?
6. What geofence radius/tolerance is appropriate for each site?
7. Which image formats, maximum file size and retention period are allowed?
8. Which decisions require one reviewer versus two-person approval?
9. What is the escalation path for reviewer disagreement?
10. Which evidence fields may be publicly verified?
11. What is the data retention, deletion and legal-hold policy?
12. May evidence be processed by third-party AI services, or must models run privately?
13. What languages must OCR support?
14. What are the target devices and connectivity conditions?
15. What operational availability, RPO and RTO are required?
16. Which applicable engineering standards and acceptance criteria will be supplied by qualified experts?
17. Is offline capture required in the MVP?
18. What is the expected number of projects, users, images per day and average image size?

Do not invent answers. Record decisions and update the relevant documents.
